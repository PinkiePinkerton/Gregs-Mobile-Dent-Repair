2. Mobile Service & Virtual Estimates

Greg's Mobile Dent Repair provides specialized on-site paintless dent removal (PDR) for cars, trucks, SUVs, vans, and RVs throughout the Phoenix East Valley, Arizona.

  • Virtual Estimates: Online quotes and estimates provided via photo or phone are preliminary assessments based on visual evidence provided. In rare cases where hidden bracing, double-walled metal, or underlying structural distortion is discovered upon physical inspection, an updated quote will be provided before work begins.
  • PDR Suitability: Paintless dent removal requires intact factory paint. If paint is cracked, broken, or has preexisting clear-coat failure, Greg will advise you on whether PDR is feasible or if body shop refinishing is required.
