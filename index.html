Common Questions About Mobile Dent Repair

PDR is a specialized technique to remove dents by carefully massaging them out from behind the panel without painting, preserving your vehicle's original finish. It's ideal for door dings, minor dents, and hail damage.

Most mobile dent repairs are completed in 30 minutes to a few hours, depending on the size and complexity of the dent.

PDR is most effective on dents where the paint is not cracked or severely damaged. I assess each dent to determine if PDR is the best solution for your car, truck, or SUV.

Often, mobile dent repair is more affordable than traditional body shop methods because it saves on paint and labor costs.
