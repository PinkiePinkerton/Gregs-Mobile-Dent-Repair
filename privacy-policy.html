Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Greg's Mobile Dent Repair ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information obtained through our website (https://gregsdent.com), phone inquiries, text messages, and mobile estimate requests.

2. Information We Collect

When you request an estimate, contact us, or schedule paintless dent removal services, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Vehicle Information: Make, model, year, and description of the damage or dent location.
  • Photographs: Any images or videos of the vehicle dent that you upload or text to us for virtual estimation purposes.
  • Location Data: Your service address, home, or workplace location in the East Valley Arizona for mobile on-site appointments.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Generate accurate virtual estimates for paintless dent repair (PDR).
  • Schedule and perform on-site mobile dent removal appointments at your designated location.
  • Communicate directly with you via phone, text (SMS), or email regarding your estimate, arrival time, or repair details.
  • Maintain customer records for invoice generation and service warranties.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal contact information with third-party marketing brokers.

4. SMS / Text Messaging Terms

By providing your telephone number on our estimate form or texting us at (602) 316-9263, you consent to receive customer-service text messages regarding your estimate, appointment scheduling, and service notifications. Standard message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying "STOP".

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement reasonable physical and digital safeguards to protect your personal details and vehicle photos against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Uploaded images are used solely for damage assessment and repair quality documentation.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may utilize standard cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel) to monitor site traffic, ensure form functionality, and optimize user experience. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies if preferred.
